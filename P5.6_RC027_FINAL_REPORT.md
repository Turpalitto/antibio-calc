## 1. RC-027 root cause
`ReviewService.packet()` read `item.get("original_text") or item.get("source_quote")` off
`snapshot["provenance"]` dict entries. Neither key is ever produced by the `ClinicalRegimen`/
`TherapeuticOption` provenance builders (which spread `FieldProvenance` — fields
`source_object_id`/`source_document`/`source_location`/`source_store`/`scope`, no text field at
all) nor by the golden-case provenance shape (`author`/`verified_at`/`source`). The target
object's OWN `source_quote` field was populated the whole time; `packet()` simply never read it.
Full detail: `RC027_ROOT_CAUSE_REPORT.md`.
